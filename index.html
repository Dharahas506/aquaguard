🔬 What is Turbidity?

Turbidity measures how cloudy or unclear water is because of tiny particles suspended in it.

Higher turbidity generally means more particles are present and the water looks cloudier.

Monitoring turbidity can help identify changes in water quality and indicate when further testing may be needed.

Important: Turbidity alone cannot tell us whether water is safe to drink. Other tests are needed too.
